How it gets there

Somebody drives over and installs it.

Not a login. Not a trial. A machine on your network, wired to the files you already have, set up by the person who will also be the one running it.

  1. We come to you

    One day on site, in your building, with you in the room. Wake County and the Triangle — close enough to come back when something needs a hand.

  2. It gets wired to your actual business

    Your spreadsheets, your customer database, your job files, your email. Not a blank product you then have to feed. It already knows how your shop works, because we point it at how your shop works.

  3. Nothing is typed by hand

    Every step is a script that checks its own work and writes down what it did. You double-click a file and read a plain verdict. If a step can't verify itself, it stops instead of guessing. That's the difference between a setup that works and one that only looks like it worked.

  4. Then we run it

    It gets watched, corrected and improved every month. When it breaks, you call one person and that person already knows your setup.

Why this matters more than the software: anyone can sell you a subscription to an AI tool. Not many will drive out, put it on your network, connect it to your files, and pick up the phone when it stops working on a Friday. That's really the whole offer.

The question everybody asks

“So does my customer list end up at an AI company?”

Fair question, and the honest answer has two halves.

Stays put

Your files stay on your machine

The customer list, the job history, the pricing, the documents — those live on the box in your building, the same as they do now. We do not copy your business onto somebody else's server to make this work.

Goes out, on purpose

The thinking happens at Claude

When the agent needs to actually reason about something, it sends that piece — and only that piece — to Anthropic's Claude and gets an answer back. That part is not local, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you something slower and worse.

Yours, not ours

The account is in your name

We set it up under your Anthropic account, with a spending cap you choose. You see every dollar. You can change the password, cut our access, or shut the whole thing off without asking us. We'd rather you have that button than not.

Everything it sends is written down. There is a log of every request, on your machine, that you can read. If you ever want to know what it looked at on a Tuesday, that's a question with an answer, not a shrug.
